# Content rules for the Olivo site

These apply to every string in `content/`. They exist because this is a medical practice in Illinois and Dr. Olivo is personally accountable for the claims.

## Banned
- "FDA-approved" or "FDA-cleared" unless the exact indication is quoted and sourced. Prefer "used for" language.
- "permanent", "cure", "guaranteed", "risk-free", "painless", "safe for everyone", "best", "#1", "top rated", "revolutionary", "miracle".
- "rejuvenation" for any energy device and anything with "vaginal" in it. Use "pelvic floor strengthening" and "intimate wellness" only with cleared-indication wording.
- Any percentage improvement, fat-reduction figure, or muscle-gain figure unless it carries a `verify` note naming the manufacturer study.
- Any price, APR, "0% interest", "no interest" or "same as cash". Financing copy is only: "Financing available through Cherry. Terms are set by Cherry at application."
- Before and after results, patient names, ages, or initials.
- Claims that Dr. Olivo owned, built, or sold another business, or that she is board certified in a specialty other than what `content/clinic.ts` states.
- Em dashes. Use a period, a comma, or a colon.
- Superlatives about the clinic versus other clinics.

## Required
- Every treatment page states that candidacy and expectations are set at a consultation.
- "Individual results vary" appears once per treatment page (the template adds it).
- Anything unsourced goes in `verify` so it renders as `<!-- VERIFY -->` in the HTML.
- Tone: confident, specific, peer-level. Second person. Short sentences. No exclamation marks.
- Product names as the manufacturer writes them: Emsculpt Neo, Emface, Exion, Emsella, Emfemme 360, Opus Plasma, Opus Colibri, Miradry, Hydrafacial, Botox Cosmetic, Xeomin, Revanesse Versa, Belotero, Radiesse, Kybella, Alastin, ZO Skin Health, Skinbetter Science.
