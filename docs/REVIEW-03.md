# REVIEW-03: The Precision

Screenshots at 390, 834 and 1440 in all five palettes, the Platform index with a row focused, the open mobile nav, the footer and the interior page.

## Round 1

1. Downtime tags wrapped onto two lines inside the index rows ("A few days"), which broke the instrument-panel rhythm. Fixed: tags never wrap.
2. The twelve drafting column rules ran at 390px too, where they read as noise behind the count-up numbers. Fixed: column rules render from the tablet breakpoint up; the section keeps its top and bottom hairlines on mobile.
3. SaaS risk check: the proof strip is four numbers on rules, no icons; the index is a table, not cards; the concern results are an indexed three-column list with hairlines; the reviews are an indexed list. No rounded corners above 2px anywhere. Photography (skin and steel placeholders, the room) carries the warmth; the glacier ground is warmed ice, not cool grey.
4. Keyboard: index rows are `role="option"` buttons with ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Home and End; focus pins the drawing and spec card. Segmented controls are tab lists.

## Round 2

- Anti-template: numbered indices are Precision's one shared-vocabulary device, plus its own drawn hairlines. No grain, marquee, cursor or SplitText. Two eyebrow stacks maximum (hero and memberships); other sections open with numbers, a portrait, a table, a control, an image, a map, a list.
- Luxury: one hero message and one CTA; physician immediately after the numbers; devices named and drawn as objects; specific facts as typography.
- Headline cadence varies: "Every platform. One physician." / "Platform index" / "Start with the concern" / "Results, side by side" / "Two memberships, one for skin and one for body." / "Book a consultation with Dr. Olivo's team."
- Would the client mistake it for another concept? No: the only concept with a monospace data layer, a split hero, and a table.

Open items: none.
