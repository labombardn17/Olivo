# REVIEW-04: The Residence

Screenshots at 390, 834 and 1440 in all five palettes, the rooms with a hotspot open, the open mobile nav, the footer and the interior page.

## Round 1

1. The "Enter" scroll cue collided with the hero CTA row inside the frame. Fixed: CTAs sit higher (pb 80px mobile, 112px desktop) so the cue has its own space at the frame's foot.
2. Hotspot check with motion on: Lenis smooth scroll made the Playwright hover land at the frame edge; verified the open state under reduced motion instead. Card opens to the right of the marker, or to the left ("flip") when the marker sits past 60% of the frame width, so it never leaves the photograph.
3. Atelier versus Residence: Residence is framed (24px inset hairline on every panel), symmetrical (centered wordmark, centered headlines, split panels), and photography-led (display capped at 3vw; hero, rooms, concern, results and the interior page are image first). Atelier is bled, asymmetric and type-led at 8 to 12vw. No shared devices: Residence uses no grain, no marquee, no numbered indices as a design device, no cursor and no SplitText.
4. Stock cliche check: no stones, towels, orchids or cucumber. Placeholders are rooms, light, hands and instruments.

## Round 2

- Anti-template: symmetry is the concept, so centered composition is intentional here and nowhere else. Sections are separated by frames and 96 to 240px of air, not boxes with shadows. Two eyebrow stacks maximum (rooms and the physician); other sections open with the facts, a portrait, a question, an image, two membership names, a map.
- Luxury: the physician immediately after the proof line; devices discovered inside rooms; one silent section (results: an image and one line); Ken Burns on the poster while the film loads; gentle 20px drifts and 120ms line staggers; the only hover zoom is 1.03 on room images.
- Headline cadence varies: a long sentence with a finite verb (hero), "Nine platforms, shown where they stand, in the rooms on Fullerton." / "What would you like to change?" / "Come as you are. Leave as you intend."

Open items: none. Room names and hotspot positions are placeholders until room photography is made (noted in ASSETS.md).
