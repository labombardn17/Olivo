# DECISIONS.md

Decisions made without asking, in the order they came up, then one paragraph per concept.

## Build decisions

1. **Standalone app in `olivo/`.** The parent repository is the Benchmark Advisors site on Next 14 and Tailwind 3 and owns `/`. The brief requires Next 15, Tailwind 4 and root-level routes, so the prototype is its own package with its own lockfile, deployable on its own. Nothing in the parent site changed.
2. **Palette resolution, and why the pages are static.** `html[data-palette]` is set by a blocking inline script before first paint (URL param, then localStorage, then the concept default read from the pathname), the next-themes pattern with `suppressHydrationWarning` on `<html>`. The switcher's hook (`useSearchParams` inside a Suspense boundary) applies `?palette=` on client navigations. The first build awaited `searchParams` in every page as the brief describes; that made every route dynamic, and Next 15.5 streams `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` into the body for dynamic routes when the user agent is not a known HTML-limited bot, which fails Lighthouse's SEO audit and costs performance. The inline script already gives a zero-flash first paint for all three cases, so the pages are prerendered and the server-side read was dropped. `/compare` reads its `a`, `b`, `pa`, `pb` params on the client for the same reason.
3. **Concept switch.** `TransitionOverlay` lives in the root layout, wipes up in the incoming palette's ground (400ms), then `router.push`, waits for the new segment to mount by watching the pathname, then reveals. `router.prefetch` fires on hover and focus of a concept in the switcher. If the client has chosen a palette it is carried in the URL so the deep link stays shareable.
4. **Fonts per segment.** Each concept declares its faces in `(concepts)/<name>/layout.tsx` and maps them to `--font-display-face`, `--font-text-face` and `--font-mono-face` on the wrapper. Tailwind `font-display` and friends resolve through `@theme inline`, so no concept CSS names a family. Geist Mono is declared in the switcher module only.
5. **Layers.** Base resets sit in `@layer base`, helper classes (`.tint`, `.u-draw`, `.marquee`) and concept CSS in `@layer components`, so Tailwind utilities always win and nothing needs `!important` except a handful of deliberate overrides.
6. **Placeholders are synthetic.** Pexels and Coverr were blocked from the build environment, and the brief forbids aerials of another city, so the drone placeholder is a generated 12 second pan with grain. All photography placeholders are generated tonal fields. No stock imagery anywhere.
7. **Device silhouettes.** One set of six abstract shapes (console, arm, handpiece, chair, bed, tower) shared by all five placeholder treatments, so real photography swaps in at the same 4:5 ratio in every concept. Nothing resembles a trademarked product.
8. **Board certification.** The line reads "Certified in aesthetic medicine by the American Board of Aesthetic Medicine" with the counsel VERIFY comment. The client's site does not name the board; the name is a placeholder for counsel to confirm or replace.
9. **Memberships, hours, transit, Instagram handle, first-visit steps, downtime tags, concern mapping.** None are in the brief. Each carries a VERIFY marker and is listed in `docs/VERIFY.md`.
10. **Access control.** No Vercel CLI in this environment, so password protection could not be enabled from here. `middleware.ts` implements an optional `OLIVO_ACCESS_TOKEN` gate (`?key=` once, then a cookie). Every route is noindex, nofollow with an `X-Robots-Tag` header as well.
11. **Screenshots under reduced motion.** Full-page captures run with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` so scroll-triggered reveals do not leave sections invisible; hero stills and the signature moments are captured with motion on. The script scrolls through each page first so lazy images load.
12. **Playwright Chromium.** The environment's pre-installed Chromium is used via `executablePath`; Lighthouse uses the same binary through chrome-launcher.

## Concept 01: The Atelier

The idea: a printed monograph on the clinic, type as the image, paper and ink. Fraunces at 8 to 12vw, Manrope small caps, a 12-column asymmetric grid with headlines at column 2 and body at 7 to 11, running heads spelled in words and folios in the margin, grain at 5%. The signature moment is "The Instruments": a pinned two-column spread where the device name and a spelled ordinal step through the nine while an engraved plate crossfades and settles from 1.15 to 1.0. It could only be for Olivo because the plates are the nine machines she actually owns, in the order she uses them, and the prologue is her line, "aging is optional." Build estimate to a full site: 5 to 6 weeks, mid cost, most of it in typographic templates for treatment pages. Photography needed: portrait, three editorial skin and hands images, and 4:5 device shots on paper-white. If chosen, build next: the treatment index page as a catalogue raisonne with one plate per treatment.

## Concept 02: The Cinema

The idea: a dark screening room where the devices are sculpture under one light. Instrument Sans tight at display size, Instrument Serif italic for one accent word and the pull quotes, micro caps at 11px. The film shrinks into a letterbox on scroll. The signature moment is "The Collection": a pinned horizontal gallery, one device per panel with a 1 / 9 counter, a custom cursor that reads Drag, and drag-to-scroll. It could only be for Olivo because "Aging is optional" is her line and the collection is her inventory. Build estimate: 6 to 7 weeks, higher cost, driven by object photography and the pinned gallery's device pages. Photography needed: dark-ground object photography of all nine devices (the concept depends on it), a portrait lit the same way, and a sound-on film cut. If chosen, build next: the device pages as single-object stages with the film cut per device.

## Concept 03: The Precision

The idea: a Swiss clinic that sells the machines with data. Schibsted Grotesk in two weights, JetBrains Mono for every label and spec, a drafting grid with visible column rules, split-screen layouts, corners never above 2px. The signature moment is the "Platform index": an instrument-panel table of the nine with a filter by area, keyboard navigation, and a pinned technical drawing and spec card that follow the focused row. It could only be for Olivo because the table is a real inventory that most competitors cannot fill. Build estimate: 4 to 5 weeks, lowest cost, because the system is a table and a grid. Photography needed: skin and steel close-ups, a hard-lit portrait, and manufacturer product shots on neutral ground. If chosen, build next: per-device spec pages generated from the same content model, with treatment-area filtering across the whole menu.

## Concept 04: The Residence

The idea: a boutique hotel in the trees; image is the type. Cormorant Garamond light capped at 3vw, Figtree text, a 1px frame inset 24px from the viewport on every panel, centered and symmetrical, 96 to 240px of air between panels. The signature moment is "The rooms": full-frame room photographs with numbered hotspots that open into device name and function, then a quiet indexed list and a five-step first-visit timeline. It could only be for Olivo because the machines are discovered inside her rooms on Fullerton rather than on a spec sheet. Build estimate: 5 to 6 weeks, mid to high cost, almost all of it photography and art direction. Photography needed: three to five rooms shot in the same light (the concept does not work without them), a portrait, hands and instruments. If chosen, build next: the rooms as a navigable floor plan, one page per room.

## Concept 05: The Current

The idea: bold, contemporary, moving; color blocks alternating ground and inverse ground, content that breaks the container. Archivo at 125% width for display, Onest for text, hero at 8.5 to 15vw. The film starts in a tall frame and expands to full bleed on scroll to become the ground of the proof strip. The signature moment is "Nine machines. One clinic.": a sticky counter from 01 to 09 beside cards that peel over one another, each a flat block with a cut-out silhouette, and a marquee of device names beneath. It could only be for Olivo because the count is her nine, and "Built different, on Fullerton" is a line no franchise can say. Build estimate: 5 to 6 weeks, mid cost, motion work is the long pole. Photography needed: bold, high-contrast skin and clinic images that hold up as 4-up grids, a portrait that can overlap blocks, and device shots that cut out cleanly. If chosen, build next: the treatment pages as full-bleed color-block stories with the counter carried across the site.

## Performance notes (recorded after the Lighthouse pass)

13. **LCP and the placeholder poster.** Chrome ignores images under 0.05 bits per pixel as LCP candidates, and the first synthetic poster was that smooth, so the first video frame became the LCP on Residence and Current and the simulated download of the placeholder film dragged the score below 85. Three changes, all of which a real drone frame would have made unnecessary: the poster carries visible grain, it is fetched at 150vw on portrait screens (object-fit cover only shows the middle of a landscape frame, so this is also the correct resolution), and video sources are injected on the first interaction or after 3s idle rather than 1.5s. The placeholder film is encoded at about 2 Mbps because it is soft; the README keeps the 4 to 6 Mbps target for client footage.
14. **Why the switcher loads after hydration.** The switcher and `motion` are not on the first-paint path, so `DesignSwitcher` is a `next/dynamic` import with `ssr: false`, and the transition overlay uses a CSS clip-path transition. Atelier's score sits closest to the gate because Fraunces with three axes is a 118 KB font; the brief requires those axes.
