# ASSETS.md: what is placeholder, what to replace, and licenses

Every image, video and review on the prototype is a placeholder. Nothing here is a photograph of the clinic, a patient, a device or a person.

- Generated in this repository by `scripts/make-video.mjs`: a 12 second slow pan over a soft two-tone field with film grain, 1920x1080 H.264 at about 5 Mbps plus VP9 WebM, poster pulled from frame 15. No footage of any city, so nothing misrepresents the location.
- License: none needed, synthetic.

## Photography placeholders

- `public/img/placeholders/*.jpg`, generated by `scripts/make-placeholders.mjs`: monochrome tonal fields with one light source (rooms, skin, hands, steel, light, a wall). License: none needed, synthetic.
- Each is tinted per palette at render time and labelled "Placeholder" in its alt text.
- Replace with: three treatment room photographs (Residence depends on these; hotspot positions in `components/concepts/residence/ResidenceRooms.tsx` are set per photograph), two skin detail images, one hands-at-work image, one instrument or steel detail, one wall-and-light image. Landscape 1600x1000 and portrait 1200x1500 keep the current crops.

## Device imagery

- No photograph or drawing of a trademarked device is used. Each concept renders an abstract silhouette in its own treatment (engraved plate, dark object study, technical drawing, room hotspot, flat color block). The silhouettes are generic shapes in `components/shared/DeviceSilhouette.tsx`.
- BTL (Emsculpt Neo, Emface, Exion, Emsella, Exion RF microneedling), Alma (Opus Plasma, Opus Colibri), miraDry and HydraFacial provide approved imagery to clinic customers on request through their practice marketing programs. Request 4:5 product shots on a neutral ground for Atelier, Precision and Current, and dark-ground object photography for Cinema.
- Manufacturer logos need partner-marketing permission before use. Until then, names appear as text only, and the footer carries the trademark attribution line.
